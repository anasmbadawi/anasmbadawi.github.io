Research

What I am exploring now

My academic work asks how European external action is organised, and what that means for coherence when politics changes quickly.

Question 01

Who acts?

Which EU institutions and Member States have legal and political room to act?

Question 02

How coherent is the response?

Do different actors reinforce each other, overlap, diverge, or pull in different directions?

Question 03

Does context change the pattern?

I am increasingly interested in comparison beyond the European neighbourhood, including Myanmar after 2011.

College of Europe thesis

Syria after December 2024

My recent research examined the EU response to the fall of the Assad regime and how the division of competences affected the coherence of EU reactions and actions.

The project received the College of Europe prize for an Outstanding Master’s Thesis in European Neighbourhood Studies.

From Syria to a wider comparison.

I am now asking whether the patterns I observed are specific to the neighbourhood, or whether they tell us something broader about EU external action.
