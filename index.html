01 / About

Research shaped by academic inquiry and policy practice.

I work at the intersection of European studies, international relations and policy practice, with a particular interest in how the legal, institutional and political organisation of EU external action shapes coherence during political transitions.

My background combines graduate training in European affairs and public diplomacy with professional experience in civil society governance, policy research and regional dialogue initiatives across the Mediterranean.

02 / Research

Understanding how the EU acts during moments of political transition.

Current Research
Explaining and Assessing Coherence in EU External Action during Political Transitions

A comparative project examining how legal, institutional and political organisation shapes the coherence of EU and Member State reactions and actions, with comparative attention to Myanmar after 2011 and Syria after 2024.

College of Europe Thesis · 2026
The EU’s Response to the Fall of the Assad Regime in Syria: How the Division of Competences affected the Coherence of EU Reactions and Actions

Awarded the Prize for an Outstanding Master Thesis in the field of European Neighbourhood Studies at the College of Europe.

Methods
Process tracing, legal analysis and structured comparison

Research interests include process tracing, doctrinal legal analysis, qualitative document analysis, discourse analysis, policy-cluster analysis and structured comparison.
