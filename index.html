01 / About

Research informed by both academic inquiry and policy practice.

I am a researcher and policy professional focused on European Union external action, political transitions and the European neighbourhood. My academic work examines the legal, institutional and political organisation of EU foreign policy and its implications for coherence.

I graduated from the College of Europe in Natolin with an Outstanding Thesis in the EU Neighbourhood field, following postgraduate studies in international relations, public diplomacy and cultural diplomacy.

Alongside research, I have worked across civil society, international organisations and regional initiatives, connecting evidence, policy and implementation.

02 / Research

Understanding how the EU acts during moments of political transition.

Current Research
Explaining and Assessing Coherence in EU External Action during Political Transitions

A comparative research project examining how legal, institutional and political organisation shapes the coherence of EU and Member State reactions and actions, with comparative attention to Myanmar after 2011 and Syria after 2024.

College of Europe Thesis
The EU’s Response to the Fall of the Assad Regime in Syria

Research examining how the division of competences affected coherence in EU reactions and actions toward Syria during the political transition beginning in December 2024.

Methods
Process tracing, legal analysis and structured comparison

My methodological interests include process tracing, doctrinal legal analysis, qualitative document analysis, discourse analysis, policy-cluster analysis and structured comparison.
